Free and open source

Plan, build and check your plasmids.

A free, open-source plasmid editor for Mac, Windows and Linux: maps and sequence editing, primers and cloning, and sequence verification with Sanger and whole-plasmid reads.

Your files stay on your computer, and updates install only when you choose.

Download What's new Source on GitHub

Maps and editing

Open SnapGene, GenBank, FASTA and more. Circular and linear maps, an editable two-strand sequence view, and automatic detection of common plasmid parts.

Primers and cloning

Primer3-compatible Tm, binding sites, primer design and site-directed mutagenesis, PCR product previews, simulated restriction digests, and restriction, Gibson, In‑Fusion and Golden Gate cloning.

Sequence verification

Align Sanger traces and whole-plasmid reads to your construct, group reads by clone, and call amino-acid and nucleotide mutations automatically.
